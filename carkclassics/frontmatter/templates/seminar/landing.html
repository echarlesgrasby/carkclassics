What to expect

Sessions run 90 minutes. Someone reads a short passage aloud, then we spend about an hour on one question and follow where the text leads. We end with the questions we have not resolved. Nobody lectures, and the facilitator is a fellow reader.

How we talk

  • Stay with the text. "Where do you see that?" is always a fair question.
  • Listen fully before responding.
  • Disagreement is welcome. Everyone gets a turn.
